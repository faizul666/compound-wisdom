FORMAT: QUOTE POST

Produce a QuotePost object with these fields:

- quote_text: 15-25 words. Original wisdom in the brand voice — a single,
  quietly memorable line that could stand alone on an image. It should feel
  earned and true, not slogan-like. No quotation marks inside it. It is
  ORIGINAL to Calm Money Daily, never attributed to a historical figure.
- attribution: exactly "— Calm Money Daily".
- image_background_template: choose the template that best fits the tone of the
  quote, from: warm_gradient, parchment, minimal_photo, branded_solid,
  serif_card.
- caption_body: 200-400 words. A reflective expansion of the quote. Open with a
  concrete scene or observation, develop the idea, fold in one paraphrased
  Stoic thought (in your own words, no fabricated quotation), and land on a
  calm, non-prescriptive note. At least one concrete number, example, or scene.
- closing_question: one specific question a reader could answer with a real
  personal story. Not "what do you think?".

Match the cadence of the examples: varied sentence length, occasional
one-sentence paragraph, no hype.
