FORMAT: QUOTE POST

Produce a QuotePost object with these fields:

- quote_text: a REAL, verifiable quote (8-30 words) from a book or a well-known
  author/thinker in the self-improvement, business, psychology, or money space.
  It must be something the person actually wrote or said. No quotation marks
  inside the field — just the words. If you cannot be confident a quote is
  verbatim, DO NOT invent one; pick a different, well-known quote you are sure of.
- attribution: the real author and source, formatted like
  "— James Clear, Atomic Habits" or "— Charlie Munger". Always start with "— ".
- image_background_template: choose the mood that fits, from: warm_gradient,
  parchment, minimal_photo, branded_solid, serif_card.
- caption_body: 150-320 words. Open with a concrete scene or a sharp
  observation, unpack why the quote is true (with an example or number), and
  land on something the reader can use. Reference the author/book naturally.
- closing_question: one specific question a reader could answer with a real
  personal story. Not "what do you think?".

Match the voice: clear, punchy, useful. Vary sentence length.
