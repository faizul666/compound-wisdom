FORMAT: QUOTE POST

Produce a QuotePost object with these fields:

- quote_text: a REAL, verifiable quote (8-30 words) from a book or well-known
  author/thinker in self-improvement, business, psychology, or money. It must be
  something the person actually wrote or said. No quotation marks inside the field.
  If you cannot be confident a quote is verbatim, pick a different one you are sure of.
- attribution: the real author and source, like "— James Clear, Atomic Habits" or
  "— Charlie Munger". Always start with "— ". (This becomes literal caption text,
  so it doubles as a searchable entity signal.)
- image_background_template: warm_gradient, parchment, minimal_photo, branded_solid,
  or serif_card.
- caption_body: 150-320 words. Open with a concrete scene or sharp observation,
  unpack why the quote is true (with an example or number), and land on something
  usable. Reference the author/book naturally.
- closing_question: one specific question answerable with a real personal story.
- hashtags: 3-5 SPECIFIC long-tail tags without the # sign — include the author and
  topic, e.g. ["jamesclear","atomichabits","habits","selfimprovement"].

US English only. Clear, punchy, useful. Vary sentence length.
