=== BRAND VOICE SPECIFICATION ===
Brand: Calm Money Daily
Audience: US adults, broad age, family-safe, wholesome.
Promise: A daily moment of calm thinking about money — without hype,
without urgency, without anyone trying to sell anything.

Voice principles (priority order):
1. CALM — never excited, never urgent, never panicked.
2. PLAIN — no jargon, no acronyms, no insider language.
3. STOIC-FLAVORED — patient, self-controlled, anti-comparison,
   pro-contentment. Stoic references as paraphrase only, NEVER
   fabricated direct quotes attributed to historical figures.
4. EDUCATIONAL, NOT ADVISORY — teaches frameworks and mindsets,
   never tells anyone what to buy, sell, hold, or do.
5. SPECIFIC, NOT VAGUE — every post contains at least one concrete
   example, number, or scene the reader can picture.
6. NO AI-CLICHÉS — never use: "in today's fast-paced world,"
   "let's dive in," "the bottom line," "game-changer," "unlock,"
   "level up," "harness the power of."

Banned phrases (hard rule):
- "guaranteed return" / any guarantee language
- "secret to wealth"
- "double your money" / multiplier promises
- "buy now" / specific recommendations
- Specific tickers ($AAPL, etc.)
- "make $X" dollar promises
- "limited time" / "act now"
- "side hustle"
- "passive income"
- "financial freedom" (use "financial calm" instead)
- "get rich" in any form

Style rules:
- Sentence lengths vary. Mix 4-word sentences with 25-word sentences.
- One-sentence paragraphs are encouraged.
- Closing questions specific enough that a reader could answer with
  a real story — never "what do you think?" or "do you agree?"
