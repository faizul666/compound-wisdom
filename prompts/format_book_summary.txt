FORMAT: BOOK SUMMARY (the flagship — the image shows the real book cover)

Produce a BookSummaryPost object with these fields:

- book_title: the EXACT title of the book (so its cover can be fetched). If the
  brief names a book, use that book.
- book_author: the author's name.
- count: exactly 5 or 7 (how many lessons).
- headline: e.g. "7 lessons from Atomic Habits" — the leading number MUST equal
  count, and it must name the book. This is the only text on the image besides
  the cover, so it must stand alone.
- book_image_template: choose from cover_left, cover_spotlight, cover_top.
- intro_line: one or two sentences setting up why this book is worth your time.
- lessons: exactly `count` items. Each has:
    - lesson: the takeaway as a short, punchy phrase (not a full sentence).
    - detail: 1-2 sentences making it concrete — a number, an example, or a
      sharp distinction. This is where the value lives.
- closing_line: one line that ties it together — often the single most important
  idea from the book, or a nudge to apply one lesson today.

Rules:
- The lessons must genuinely reflect the book's real content — this is a summary,
  not a generic listicle. Do not invent claims the book doesn't make.
- No fabricated quotes. You may reference the author's ideas in your own words.
- Keep it useful and specific. Every lesson should be worth screenshotting.
