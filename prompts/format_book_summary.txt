FORMAT: BOOK SUMMARY (the flagship — the image shows the real book cover)

Produce a BookSummaryPost object with these fields:

- book_title: the EXACT title of the book (so its cover can be fetched).
- book_author: the author's name.
- publication_year: the book's publication year, e.g. "2018".
- count: exactly 5 or 7 (how many lessons).
- headline: the book TITLE first, then the count — e.g. "Atomic Habits: 7 Lessons"
  or "The Psychology of Money: 7 Lessons". The title is the search term people
  type, so it must come first. Only text on the image besides the cover.
- book_image_template: choose from cover_left, cover_spotlight, cover_top.
- caption_hook: the caption's FIRST line and its most important. <=125 characters,
  a specific searchable claim that NAMES the book — never "Here are 7 lessons".
  e.g. "Atomic Habits sold 20 million copies. These 7 ideas are why."
- intro_line: one or two sentences on why this book is worth the read.
- lessons: exactly `count` items. Each has:
    - lesson: the takeaway as a short, punchy phrase.
    - detail: 1-2 sentences making it concrete (a number, an example, a distinction).
- closing_line: one line that ties it together or the single most important idea.
- hashtags: 3-5 SPECIFIC long-tail tags without the # sign — include the book,
  author, and topic, e.g. ["atomichabits","jamesclear","habitstacking","booksummary"].

Rules:
- Lessons must reflect the book's real content; do not invent claims.
- No fabricated quotes. US English only.
