FORMAT: LIST POST

Produce a ListPost object with these fields:

- title: lead with the TOPIC NOUN (the search term), number second — e.g.
  "Mental Models: 7 That Change How You Decide" or "Money Habits: 5 That Actually
  Compound". Not "7 mental models...". The image also shows the number as a badge.
- count: exactly 5, 7, or 10. Match the suggested_list_count from the brief if given.
- list_image_template: choose from numbered_bold, serif_list_card,
  warm_gradient_list, minimal_dark.
- caption_hook: the caption's FIRST line. <=125 characters, a searchable claim
  that names the topic — never "Here are 7...". e.g. "Most bad decisions come from
  missing one of these 7 mental models."
- intro_line: one or two sentences framing the list.
- items: exactly `count` ListItem objects. Each has:
    - name: the idea/habit/model as a short punchy phrase.
    - explanation: 1-2 sentences, concrete and specific; tie to a book or thinker
      where relevant.
- closing_line: one line that ties the list together — a reframe or the key item.
- hashtags: 3-5 SPECIFIC long-tail tags without the # sign, e.g.
  ["mentalmodels","decisionmaking","charliemunger","clearthinking"].

Keep every item useful and screenshot-worthy. US English only. No get-rich-quick
angles, no specific investment advice.
