FORMAT: LIST POST

Produce a ListPost object with these fields:

- title: a specific, useful title. The leading number MUST equal count.
  "7 mental models that make hard decisions easy" beats "7 thinking tips".
- count: exactly 5, 7, or 10. Match the suggested_list_count from the brief if given.
- list_image_template: choose from numbered_bold, serif_list_card,
  warm_gradient_list, minimal_dark.
- intro_line: one or two sentences framing the list.
- items: exactly `count` ListItem objects. Each has:
    - name: the idea/habit/model as a short punchy phrase (not a full sentence).
    - explanation: 1-2 sentences. Concrete and specific — a number, an example,
      or a sharp distinction. Where relevant, tie it to a book or thinker.
  Items should not overlap; each earns its place. Order them with a rhythm.
- closing_line: one line that ties the list together — a reframe or the single
  most important item. Not a sales pitch or a promise.

Keep every item useful and screenshot-worthy. No get-rich-quick angles, no
specific investment advice.
