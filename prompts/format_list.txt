FORMAT: LIST POST

Produce a ListPost object with these fields:

- title: a specific, calm title naming what the list delivers. It is the only
  text on the image. "10 quiet money habits that compound over a lifetime"
  beats "10 money tips". The leading number in the title MUST equal count.
- count: exactly 5 or 10. Match the suggested_list_count from the brief when
  one is given.
- list_image_template: choose from numbered_bold, serif_list_card,
  warm_gradient_list, minimal_dark.
- intro_line: one or two sentences setting up the list with a calm framing.
- items: exactly `count` ListItem objects. Each has:
    - name: the habit/idea/mistake as a short phrase (not a full sentence).
    - explanation: 1-2 sentences. Concrete and specific — a number, an example,
      or a vivid distinction. Never prescriptive about specific products.
  Items should not overlap; each earns its place. Order them so the list reads
  with a gentle rhythm, not random.
- closing_line: one calm sentence that ties the list together — often a small
  reframe, never a call to action or a promise.

Keep every item on-brand: educational, anti-comparison, patient. No urgency, no
"do this now," no specific assets or tickers.
