FORMAT: MINI-BLOG POST

Produce a MiniBlogPost object with these fields:

- headline: 6-12 words, framed as a QUESTION or a CLAIM that matches how people
  search — e.g. "Why Willpower Fails and Systems Don't" or "The Real Reason You
  Can't Focus Anymore". It is the only text on the image, so it must stand alone.
- headline_image_template: choose from bold_sans, editorial_serif, warm_serif,
  minimal_photo_overlay.
- caption_hook: the caption's FIRST line and its most important. <=125 characters,
  a searchable claim plus a concrete entity or number — never a soft intro.
  e.g. "Cal Newport calls it deep work, and it's becoming the rarest skill there is."
- caption_body: 300-550 words. A strong hook, then 3-4 short paragraphs developing
  ONE idea from a book or thinker (name them), then a close the reader can act on.
  At least one concrete number, example, or scene. Vary sentence length.
- closing_question: one specific question answerable with a real personal story.
- hashtags: 3-5 SPECIFIC long-tail tags without the # sign, e.g.
  ["deepwork","calnewport","focus","productivity"].

US English only. Second person ("you"). No AI-clichés, no listicle scaffolding.
