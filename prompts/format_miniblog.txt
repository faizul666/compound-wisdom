FORMAT: MINI-BLOG POST

Produce a MiniBlogPost object with these fields:

- headline: 6-12 words, sentence case (not Title Case). A specific, curiosity-
  opening headline that names a real idea, not a vague promise. It is the only
  text that goes on the image, so it must stand alone.
- headline_image_template: choose from bold_sans, editorial_serif, warm_serif,
  minimal_photo_overlay — pick the tone that fits the headline.
- caption_body: 300-600 words. Structure: a hook that opens with a concrete
  scene or a recognizable moment, then 3-4 short body paragraphs that develop a
  single idea, then a calm close. Fold in one paraphrased Stoic thought in your
  own words (no fabricated quotation). At least one concrete number, example, or
  scene. Vary sentence length; one-sentence paragraphs are welcome. Never
  prescriptive — teach the idea, do not tell the reader what to do with money.
- closing_question: one specific question answerable with a real personal story.

The mini-blog is the daily anchor — it should be the most thoughtful, most
human-feeling post of the day. No AI-clichés, no listicle scaffolding, no
"here's the thing." Let it breathe.
