FORMAT: MINI-BLOG POST

Produce a MiniBlogPost object with these fields:

- headline: 6-12 words, sentence case (not Title Case). A specific,
  curiosity-opening headline that names a real idea. It is the only text on the
  image, so it must stand alone. Often built around one book's key idea.
- headline_image_template: choose from bold_sans, editorial_serif, warm_serif,
  minimal_photo_overlay.
- caption_body: 300-550 words. Structure: a strong hook (a scene, a surprising
  claim, or a question), then 3-4 short body paragraphs that develop ONE idea
  from a book or thinker, then a close the reader can act on. Name the book/
  author. At least one concrete number, example, or scene. Vary sentence length;
  one-sentence paragraphs welcome. Teach the idea — never push a product or
  promise an outcome.
- closing_question: one specific question answerable with a real personal story.

The mini-blog is the deepest post of the day — the most human, most useful.
No AI-clichés, no listicle scaffolding, no "here's the thing." Let it breathe.
